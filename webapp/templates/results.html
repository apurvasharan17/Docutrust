The fix: fusing input-side signals

A logistic regression combines {{ fusion.features | length }} signals — logprob, OCR confidence, a second OCR engine, business-rule validation, document OCR quality — into one confidence. Scored on fields where the model returned a value; folds are split by document, so no test invoice is ever seen in training.
